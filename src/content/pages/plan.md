## A plan, not a lead form

The interactive planner will ask for an expedition, departure season, group size, pace, and one to
three activities. Those choices will remain in the page URL so a plan can survive refresh and be
shared without an account or database.

Northstar will not ask for personal or payment information. The result will combine the selected
journey's fixed duration and per-person starting price with a clear list of exclusions. It will not
represent live availability, a quote, or a booking.

## Current availability

The content foundation is available now. Interactive planning will be implemented in a later stage
and will include invalid-input, direct-link, history, reset, copy, and print checks.
