## Travel with a clear point of view

Northstar presents a small fictional collection of journeys for people who want to spend more time
inside a landscape than moving past it. Each expedition has a fixed route, a bounded group size, and
enough unhurried time to understand where the trail, coast, or desert leads.

The journeys and prices on this site are illustrative portfolio content. Northstar is not a travel
operator and cannot accept bookings.

## Start with the journey

Browse the complete expedition collection to compare season, duration, activity, and difficulty.
Every detail page explains the route and the assumptions behind its indicative price.

## Plan without surrendering your inbox

The planned trip builder will use a small set of non-sensitive choices. It will not ask for a name,
email address, exact travel date, health information, or payment details. A completed plan will be a
shareable summary, not a booking request.
