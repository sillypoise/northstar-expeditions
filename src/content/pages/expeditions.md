## A deliberately small collection

These journeys are designed as distinct editorial and planning examples rather than live travel
inventory. Seasons describe broad departure windows, and prices are fixed illustrative starting
points per person in US dollars.

Choose a journey to read its route, daily shape, inclusions, and practical limits. Combine filters
to narrow the collection, or reset them to see every journey. Without JavaScript, the complete
collection remains readable and every detail link still works.
