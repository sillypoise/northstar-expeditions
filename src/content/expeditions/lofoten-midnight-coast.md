## Journey outline

This fictional eight-day route begins with a ferry crossing from Bodø and follows the outer edge of
the Lofoten archipelago. Travel between bases is kept short so weather windows can shape each day
without turning the itinerary into a race.

### Reine and the western islands

The first base supports a sheltered-water paddle and a ridge walk selected for the group's pace. The
long evening light leaves room to wait out passing rain rather than forcing a fixed departure.

### Nusfjord on foot

A coastal transfer leads to two walking days around Nusfjord. One day follows old paths above the
harbor; the other remains flexible for photography, a shorter shoreline walk, or rest.

### Svolvær and departure

The route closes near Svolvær with one final paddle if conditions allow. A land-based walk replaces
it when wind or sea state crosses the guide's operating boundary.

## Illustrative inclusions

- Seven nights in shared twin accommodation.
- Local ground and ferry transport described by the route.
- Guided walks and paddles with activity equipment.
- Breakfast and trail lunch.

Travel to Bodø, dinner, insurance, and real availability are not included or verified.
