## Journey outline

This fictional eight-day route uses three bases west of Calgary. Each walking day has a shorter
alternative, and no route depends on wildlife proximity or a summit outcome.

### Kananaskis

The first two walks build from a valley route to an open viewpoint. The guide changes trail choice
when weather, fire restrictions, or wildlife closures affect the planned area.

### Yoho

A two-night stay reduces transfer time and supports one longer walking day. The route remains on
maintained non-technical trails and uses established access points.

### Banff close

The final base provides a weather-selected ridge or valley walk before returning to Calgary. Busy
trailhead timing is treated as an operating constraint rather than hidden from the itinerary.

## Illustrative inclusions

- Seven nights in shared twin accommodation.
- Ground transport from and to Calgary.
- Guided non-technical day walks.
- Breakfast and packed lunch on walking days.

Travel to Calgary, park entry changes, dinner, insurance, wildlife sightings, and real availability
are not included or guaranteed.
