## Journey outline

This fictional ten-day route crosses Hokkaido from the central highlands to the eastern wetlands.
Walking days alternate with shorter cultural and transfer days so the journey does not treat every
location as a checklist.

### Daisetsuzan highlands

Two moderate walks explore volcanic plateaus and lower forest. Routes are selected each morning
within fixed time and elevation limits because wind and early cold can change conditions quickly.

### Akan-Mashu

The middle stay combines lakeside paths with time to learn about the region's living Ainu culture
through an established local cultural program. The itinerary must not present culture as scenery or
claim access beyond that public program.

### Eastern wetlands

The final days slow down near Kushiro. Boardwalk and riverside routes provide a lower-impact close
to the journey before the return connection.

## Illustrative inclusions

- Nine nights in shared twin accommodation.
- Rail and ground transport described by the route.
- Guided non-technical walks and listed cultural entry.
- Breakfast and selected regional dinners.

International travel, personal rail changes, insurance, and real availability are not included or
verified.
