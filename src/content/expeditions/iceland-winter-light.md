## Journey outline

This fictional seven-day winter route stays on Iceland's south coast and treats road conditions as a
hard boundary. It includes two multi-night bases rather than a daily hotel change.

### Vík coast

Short daylight outings cover coastal geology and a guide-selected walking route. Wind and road
closures can replace a planned location with a safer nearby option.

### Skaftafell base

The eastern base allows one full walking day and one flexible day. Any glacier-edge activity stays
with a qualified guide and does not imply unrestricted glacier travel.

### Evening light

Two evenings are reserved for sky observation when conditions allow. The itinerary makes no aurora
promise and provides a short indoor photography session when cloud removes the night option.

## Illustrative inclusions

- Six nights in shared twin accommodation.
- Ground transport within the listed route.
- Guided winter walks and introductory photography sessions.
- Breakfast and warm drinks during field sessions.

Flights, technical clothing, insurance, aurora sightings, and real availability are not included or
guaranteed.
