## Journey outline

This fictional nine-day route treats changing light as the itinerary's central constraint. Driving
is bounded to daylight hours, and the two longest transfers are separated by multi-night stays.

### Atlantic edge

The journey opens near Walvis Bay, where coastal fog and wetland habitat create a quieter beginning
than the interior desert. Wildlife observation stays on established access routes.

### Dune country

Two early starts around Sesriem make room for rest during the hottest part of the day. The route
does not depend on climbing the highest dune or reaching a single crowded viewpoint.

### NamibRand nights

The final desert base provides two evenings for unaided observation and introductory night
photography. Cloud, wind, and moonlight remain natural variables rather than guaranteed conditions.

## Illustrative inclusions

- Eight nights in shared twin accommodation.
- Ground transport with daylight driving limits.
- Guided landscape and wildlife sessions.
- Breakfast and dinner at remote stays.

Flights, specialist camera equipment, insurance, and real wildlife or sky conditions are not
included or guaranteed.
