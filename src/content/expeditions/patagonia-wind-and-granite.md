## Journey outline

This fictional twelve-day route joins two trekking areas without promising that Patagonian weather
will follow a schedule. A weather day near El Chaltén protects the central walking days and gives
the group a useful recovery margin.

### Torres del Paine

Three walking days move from open steppe toward the granite towers. Daily distance is adjusted
within the stated demanding grade, but the route does not include technical climbing or glacier
travel.

### Across the steppe

A full transfer day connects Chile and Argentina. Border timing and road conditions can reduce the
evening program, so no activity is promised after arrival in El Calafate.

### El Chaltén

The final base supports two long valley walks and one flexible weather day. The group chooses the
strongest forecast rather than assigning the longest walk to a fixed calendar date.

## Illustrative inclusions

- Eleven nights in shared twin accommodation.
- Ground transport between the listed route points.
- Guided non-technical trekking.
- Breakfast and packed lunch on walking days.
